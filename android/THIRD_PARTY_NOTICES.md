# Third-party assets

The `ic_mail`, `ic_lock`, `ic_arrow`, `ic_check`, `ic_visibility`, and
`ic_visibility_off` vector drawables in `app/src/main/res/drawable/` are
Material Symbols Rounded (24 px) from Google's
[material-design-icons repository](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/symbols/android).
They are licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/LICENSE).
